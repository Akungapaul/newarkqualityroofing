## 0. GLOBAL CORRECTIONS — read before writing any of the 10 pages

These override looser phrasings in older packs. Carry verbatim.

1. **Flashing "90–95% of leaks" stays HEDGED.** The verbatim 90–95% figure is a
   trade-consensus estimate, NOT a primary NRCA dataset. Use the established gold-
   exemplar framing ONLY: *"the roofing industry estimates that roughly 90–95% of
   roof leaks originate at flashing details, an industry estimate attributed to the
   NRCA."* Never state it as a precise primary NRCA statistic. ("The majority of
   leaks originate at flashing/transitions" is also acceptable.)
2. **Drip-edge fastener spacing = not more than 12 inches O.C., per IRC R905.2.8.5.**
   (NOT 8–10 in.) Drip edge extends ≥2 in onto the deck and ≥¼ in below the deck/
   fascia, with ≥2 in end laps. If 8–10 in is wanted, present it as a contractor
   best-practice tightening, attributed to GAF — never as the IRC requirement.
3. **Newark/Essex County is IRC Climate Zone 4–5, NOT 6–8.** Do NOT claim the area
   qualifies for the IRC 1/300 reduced-ventilation ratio on the cold-climate
   vapor-retarder basis. Design to **1/150** locally (IRC R806.2); mention 1/300
   only as the general code exception, not as Newark's entitlement.
4. **NJ enforces the 2021 IRC** via the NJ Uniform Construction Code (N.J.A.C. 5:23).
   So the ice-barrier 24-in-inside-wall rule AND the 8:12+ 36-in-up-slope add-on
   BOTH apply in NJ. (The 2024 IRC dropped the 36-in rule, but NJ has not adopted it.)
5. **No manufacturer-certification claims for NQR.** Naming a brand NQR installs is
   fine (VELUX, Fakro, Englert, GAF products). Claiming "VELUX certified installer",
   "Fakro certified", "LeafFilter certified", "GAF Master Elite", "Air Vent certified",
   "NRCA-certified", etc. is BANNED unless verified.
6. **No "maintenance-free / clog-proof / never clean again / lifetime no-leak"
   absolutes.** No invented failure-share percentages ("X% of leaks…", "70–85%
   capacity loss"). No fabricated document/bulletin numbers. Only ASTM D1970, ASTM
   D226, and the verified IRC sections below may be cited by number.
7. **Ice dams: root cause = attic heat loss / air leakage, not gutters.** (Building
   Science Digest 135 / John Straube; University of Minnesota Extension.) Gutters
   only aggravate eave backup. Never write "clogged gutters cause ice dams."

**Verified code sections (NJ-adopted via N.J.A.C. 5:23):**
- **IRC R806.2** — attic min net free ventilating area 1/150 (1/300 only with vapor
  retarder + 40–50% high/within-3-ft-of-ridge, cold-zone leg N/A in Newark).
- **IRC R905.1.2** — ice barrier ≥24 in inside exterior wall line (≥36 in along slope
  on ≥8:12 roofs, still in force under 2021 IRC in NJ).
- **IRC R905.2.8.5** — drip edge: ≥2 in onto deck, ≥¼ in below deck, ≤12 in O.C.,
  ≥2 in laps; required at eaves and rakes.
- **IRC R903.2.1** — flashing at roof-wall intersections; kickout/diverter where a
  sloped eave meets a vertical sidewall.
- **IRC R1003.20** — chimney cricket/saddle required where chimney width parallel to
  the ridge exceeds 30 in (and chimney doesn't intersect the ridge).
- **IRC R308.6.8** — skylights on roofs < 3:12 slope sit on a curb ≥4 in above the
  roof plane (unless mfr instructions specify otherwise).
- **IRC R908** — reroofing: a water-soaked/deteriorated deck must be removed/repaired;
  recover not permitted over an unsound base.
- **IRC R803.2 / Table R503.2.1.1(1)** — wood structural panel roof sheathing spans;
  panels <½ in over rafters >20 in O.C. need H-clips/T&G/blocking. (Treat exact
  sub-numbers as approximate across editions.)
- **ASTM D1970** — self-adhering polymer-modified bitumen ice-and-water membrane
  (self-seals around fasteners). **ASTM D226** — asphalt-saturated felt (Type I #15,
  Type II #30).
