### Common repair issues
- Natural slate rarely fails as a tile; failures are typically **corroded fasteners** (tiles slide), **degraded flashing** at valleys/chimneys/dormers, **impact-broken tiles**, and **"sugaring"** (powdery surface weathering on lower-grade slate). Individual tiles can be replaced indefinitely if the deck/nailers are sound — a key slate advantage. [PRIMARY-attrib, qualitative — NRCA/NSA]
- Synthetic slate: **fastener back-out** from high thermal movement, **edge curling**, **color fading** in lower-grade polymer; often requires **proprietary fasteners** (standard nails void warranty). [SECONDARY-named, qualitative]

| Synthetic slate: replace at ~**20–25%** warped/cracked tiles (no indefinite tile-by-tile replacement) | HomeGuide/Modernize | [SECONDARY] |

## 3. Metal (standing seam and metal shingle)

### Common repair issues
- Standing seam: **fastener loosening** at structural connections, **thermal-expansion stress** on long runs (panels >100 ft need expansion zones per NRCA), **cut-edge corrosion** in coastal air, **oil-canning** (panel buckling). Concealed fasteners → fewer leaks than shingle roofs. [PRIMARY-attrib, qualitative]
- Metal shingle: **exposed-fastener back-out**, **washer-seal failure** from thermal cycling, **galvanic/dissimilar-metal corrosion** (wrong fastener metal), cut-edge corrosion. [SECONDARY, qualitative]
- **[UNVERIFIED]** the "8% of leaks," "74% fastener-related," "68% deck-movement" percentages and the specific GAF/MCA bulletin numbers.

| Rule | Source | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Standing seam: replace at **>25%** seam-connection damage or **>20%** panel corrosion | industry consensus | [SECONDARY] |
| Metal shingle: lower threshold, **15–20%** fasteners corroded/backed-out or **>25%** panel corrosion | industry consensus | [SECONDARY] |

### Common repair issues
- Tiles can't be patched — must be **replaced with matching profile** (inventory/match problem on old roofs). Failures are mostly structural, not the tile: **foot-traffic breakage**, **moss in interlocks**, **flashing/valley deterioration**, **corroded fasteners** → tile slippage, **underlayment failure** (the real lifespan limiter). Concrete adds **spalling** from freeze-thaw and **efflorescence**. [PRIMARY-attrib (TRI/NRCA), qualitative]

### Repair-vs-replace threshold
