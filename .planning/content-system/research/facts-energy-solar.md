## 0. GLOBAL CORRECTIONS — read before writing any of the 5 pages

These override looser phrasings in the raw research and in older packs. Carry verbatim.

1. **The ENERGY STAR Roof Products program is DEAD — use CRRC, never ENERGY STAR
   for roofing.** EPA stopped certifying new roof submittals **June 1, 2021** and
   ended ENERGY STAR roof recognition/mark use **June 1, 2022**; the **CRRC-1**
   program (Cool Roof Rating Council) is the successor third-party rating system.
   There is NO active ENERGY STAR roof label. Never write "ENERGY STAR roof",
   "ENERGY STAR-rated/certified" for any roofing or coating product. (One raw
   researcher claimed the program "still exists" — that claim is WRONG and is
   OVERRIDDEN by EPA's own sunset notice, CRRC, and SPRI.)

2. **There is NO federal residential solar or insulation tax credit in 2026.**
   Under the One Big Beautiful Bill (P.L. 119-21, enacted July 4, 2025), the
   **§25D Residential Clean Energy Credit** (the "30% solar credit") is **repealed
   for any system whose installation is completed after December 31, 2025**, and
   the **§25C** insulation/air-sealing credit is **repealed for property placed in
   service after December 31, 2025**, per the IRS. NEVER tout a "30% federal tax
   credit" as available to a 2026 homeowner, and never claim the old
   30%/2032→26%/2033→22%/2034 schedule — that schedule no longer applies. If the
   credit is named at all, frame it historically ("the federal residential solar
   credit was 30% for systems completed through 2025, per the IRS"). The
   business-owned path (**§48E** Clean Electricity Investment Credit) remains for
   commercial/third-party-owned systems (see Part E). NQR is a roofer, not a tax
   advisor — attribute to "the IRS" and refer readers to a tax professional.

3. **Roof coatings add NO meaningful R-value.** A coating changes the SURFACE
   radiative properties (solar reflectance + thermal emittance), not conductive
   resistance. Energy effect comes from reflecting sunlight and lowering roof
   surface temperature — never from added insulation. Do not write "insulating
   coating", "adds R-value", or "insulates". (DOE/CRRC/RCMA define cool roofs by
   reflectance + emittance, never R-value.) The only defensible indirect framing:
   a cooler roof lets the EXISTING insulation perform nearer its rated value.

4. **NJ cool-roof claims carry the heating-climate caveat.** Newark/Essex County
   is **IRC/IECC Climate Zone 4A–5** (heating-dominated mixed climate). A
   reflective roof reduces PEAK summer cooling demand but carries a winter heating
   penalty; net annual benefit depends on insulation + climate (DOE). The ONLY
   defensible quantified cooling figure is **EPA's "reduce peak cooling demand by
   11–27%" in air-conditioned residential buildings** — and it must NOT be
   restated as "cuts your energy bill 11–27%". Surface-temperature facts: a
   reflective roof stays **>50°F cooler** than a conventional roof on a sunny
   summer afternoon (DOE); a clean white roof (80% reflective) stays **~55°F /
   31°C cooler** than a gray roof (20% reflective) (LBNL). Never promise
   "year-round savings".

5. **NJ ≠ California. Never cite Title 24 / CALGreen / CA cool-roof mandates.**
   NJ is governed by the **NJ Uniform Construction Code adopting the 2021 IECC
   (residential enforcement began April 2023)** plus the IRC/IBC/IFC and NEC.
   Prescriptive ceiling insulation for NJ's zones (4 and 5) = **R-60** (2021 IECC
   Table R402.1.3), with the R-49-over-100%-of-ceiling exception at raised-heel
   eaves. No NJ cool-roof prescriptive mandate exists for low-slope residential.

6. **No manufacturer/installer certification claims for NQR.** Naming a product
   NQR installs is fine (GAF Energy Timberline Solar, Tesla Solar Roof, CertainTeed
   Solstice, SunTegra, Gaco, Henry, Mule-Hide, GE/Momentive). Claiming a status is
   BANNED unless verified: "NABCEP certified", "GAF Energy certified", "Tesla
   certified installer", "CertainTeed credentialed", "SunTegra certified", "CRRC
   listed/certified", "ENERGY STAR certified". NABCEP certifies INDIVIDUALS (ISO/IEC
   17024), not companies; CRRC/UL ratings belong to the PRODUCT, not the installer.
   NQR may NAME these credentials/ratings; NQR may not claim to hold them.

7. **Do not pin a hard SREC-II $/MWh on any page.** NJ's solar incentive is the
   **Successor Solar Incentive (SuSI)** program's **Administratively Determined
   Incentive (ADI)**, administered by the **NJ Board of Public Utilities (NJBPU)**,
   paying SREC-IIs over a **15-year** term. The durable BPU-sourced residential
   figure was **$85/MWh** (March 6, 2023 order, in force through Energy Year
   2025-26); secondary trackers report a 2026 reduction (variously $76.50/MWh
   eff. March 6, 2026 vs $77/MWh eff. July 27, 2026) that could NOT be confirmed
   to a BPU order. State the SREC-II value QUALITATIVELY ("a fixed per-MWh SREC-II
   incentive set by the NJ Board of Public Utilities, paid over 15 years") and
   refer readers to the NJ Clean Energy Program's current rate. Do not assert a
   $/MWh number on-page.

8. **Manufacturer specs are the manufacturer's, not NQR's or independent.** Wind
   (ASTM D3161 Class F / 130 mph), hail (UL 2218 Class 4), fire (UL 790 Class A),
   wattage, and warranty percentages are the named manufacturer's published
   listings — attribute to GAF Energy / Tesla / CertainTeed / SunTegra by name,
   never as NQR-tested or independently verified.

9. **No hard cost figure has a primary source.** All solar-shingle, coating, and
   recoat-vs-replace cost ranges trace to named aggregators (EnergySage,
   SolarReviews, SolarTech Online) or manufacturers, never a primary cost
   authority. Use qualitative framing ("a fraction of tear-off/replacement cost")
   + the NQR free-estimate CTA, OR cite the range WITH the named-aggregator
   attribution. Never present a coating/solar cost as a fixed NQR price.

10. **Standard-number hygiene.** Silicone roof coatings = **ASTM D6694** (NOT
    D6511 — D6511 is a solvent-based bituminous test-methods standard). Acrylic =
    **ASTM D6083**. PV module safety = **UL 61730** (published 2017, mandatory for
    new products from Dec 4, 2019), superseding legacy **UL 1703**. Solar
    reflectance test = **ASTM C1549**; emittance = **ASTM C1371**; SRI =
    **ASTM E1980**. Do not invent in-standard minimum elongation/tensile numbers
    (paywalled) — use named manufacturer datasheet values or state qualitatively.

11. **Net metering nuance.** NJ requires net metering (N.J.S.A. 48:3-87): full
    retail (1:1) credit for exports **up to the customer's annual usage**; net
    annual SURPLUS beyond total usage is settled at the **wholesale avoided-cost**
    rate, not retail. Never write "full retail for all excess forever" or imply
    over-sizing past annual usage earns retail credit.

12. **Marketing claims to drop.** Solar panels do NOT "extend roof life" / "protect
    the shingles" as established fact (marketing — at most "panels shade the
    covering", no quantified extension). Rapid shutdown LIMITS voltage (≤30 V
    outside / ≤80 V inside the array boundary within 30 s) — it does NOT zero out
    the modules. A flashed solar mount is not "permanently leak-proof" — frame as
    "flashed and sealed per the roof manufacturer's instructions to maintain
    watertightness and warranty". Solar shingles are LESS efficient and cost MORE
    per watt than panels — frame them as an aesthetics/integration choice, never an
    efficiency or value win.

## Verified standards, codes & statutes (named — cite by number only these)

- **NEC 690.12 (NFPA 70)** — rapid shutdown for rooftop PV: conductors outside the
  array boundary ≤30 V and inside ≤80 V within 30 s; array boundary = 1 ft outside
  the array. Inside-boundary limit met by MLPE (microinverters/optimizers) or a
  listed PV Hazard Control System (**UL 3741**).
- **IRC R324.6** — rooftop PV firefighter access: pathways not less than 36 in;
  ridge setback 18 in where the array covers ≤33% of plan-view roof area, 36 in
  where >33% (2021 IRC adds higher-coverage tiers and an NFPA 13D/IRC P2904
  sprinklered-dwelling exception allowing the 18-in setback up to ~66% coverage —
  use the full tiering, verify against the NJ-adopted edition).
- **IFC 605 / 605.11** — rooftop PV must not obstruct fire-department ventilation
  (the IRC 18-in/36-in setbacks operationalize this); cite IFC 605 qualitatively
  for ventilation intent, not for a secondary "3 ft below ridge" number.
- **UL 61730-1/-2** — current PV module safety standard (superseded UL 1703;
  published 2017, mandatory for new products Dec 4, 2019).
- **UL 790 / ANSI-UL 790 (ASTM E108)** — roof-covering fire test. Since the 2013
  UL 1703 revision, PV fire Class A/B/C is assigned to the **module + mounting +
  roof-covering SYSTEM**, not the module alone (Class A most fire-resistant).
- **UL 7103** — BIPV system certification (e.g., GAF Energy Timberline Solar);
  **UL 2218** — impact/hail (Class 4 = 2-in steel ball); **ASTM D3161** — shingle
  wind (Class F ≈ 130 mph).
- **ASCE 7** (incl. ASCE 7-22 PV provisions) — wind/snow loads + ballast uplift
  for rooftop PV; corner/perimeter zones need more ballast than the field.
- **ASTM C1549** (solar reflectance, portable reflectometer) / **ASTM E903**
  (spectrophotometer) / **ASTM E1918** (field); **ASTM C1371** (thermal emittance)
  / **ASTM E408**; **ASTM E1980** (Solar Reflectance Index, SRI, 0–100 nominal).
- **ASTM D6694** — liquid-applied silicone coating for SPF roofing; **ASTM D6083**
  — liquid-applied acrylic roof coating; **ASTM D6947** — polyurethane (tensile
  floor 1,500 psi, context only); **ASTM D412 / D2370** — elastomer/coating
  tensile + elongation test methods.
- **2021 IECC Table R402.1.3** — ceiling insulation R-60 for Climate Zones 4 & 5
  (R-49 full-ceiling exception at raised-heel eaves); NJ adopted 2021 IECC,
  residential enforcement April 2023.
- **Federal tax (IRS, as amended by P.L. 119-21 / OBBB, July 4, 2025):** §25D
  residential solar credit repealed after Dec 31, 2025; §25C insulation credit
  repealed after Dec 31, 2025; §48E commercial Clean Electricity Investment Credit
  (solar facilities — placed-in-service termination after Dec 31, 2027 unless
  construction begins within 12 months of enactment); §179D whole-building
  commercial deduction (not a roof credit) repealed for construction beginning
  after June 30, 2026.
- **NJ statutes/programs:** SuSI/ADI SREC-II via **NJBPU** (P.L. 2021 c.169; Clean
  Energy Act of 2018, N.J.S.A. 48:3-87); net metering **N.J.S.A. 48:3-87**; solar
  **sales-and-use-tax exemption N.J.S.A. 54:32B-8.33** (claimed via NJ Form ST-4;
  NJ rate 6.625%); solar **property-tax exemption N.J.S.A. 54:4-3.113a/b** (claimed
  via NJ Form CRES with the municipal assessor — not automatic).

## Part D — `silicone-roof-coating` + `silicone-elastomeric-roof-coating` (commercial)
