## Named sources cited (in-text)
- **ARMA** — pressure-washing causes granule loss/premature failure; 50:50 laundry-strength chlorine-bleach-and-water at a 15–20-minute dwell + low-pressure rinse; moss lifts/curls shingle edges → wind blow-off + lateral water to deck; proper maintenance extends shingle life ~25–30%; zinc/copper inhibit growth but no strips on an existing roof (exposed nails leak / break sealant); leaf-litter conditions for colonies.
- **ARMA and Atlas Roofing** — Gloeocapsa magma is the most prevalent roof-discoloration algae, feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles.
- **GAF and InterNACHI** — granule loss >~30% of surface = common beyond-repair rule-of-thumb.
- **CSSB and NRCA** — north-facing/shaded slopes hold moisture and grow moss faster.
- **NRCA** — inspection at least twice per year (spring/fall) + after any major weather event.
- **This Old House** — cleaning $300–$1,050, avg $675 for a 1,500-sq-ft home, $0.20–$0.70/sq ft; moss-prevention treatment adds $150–$250 (cost FAQ + pricing.note).
