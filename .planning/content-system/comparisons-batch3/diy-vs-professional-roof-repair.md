## directAnswer (40 words)
**Professional roof repair** beats **DIY roof repair** on safety and durability — most ladder injuries strike homes, not job sites (per D'Souza et al.), and a pro carries the harness and HIC registration DIY lacks. DIY wins only on cost.

## verdict
- **winner:** Professional repair wins on safety, diagnosis, and warranty; DIY wins only on the $50–$300 material cost of ground-level tasks.
- **reasoning:** Comparison-Proposition — Professional over DIY when work leaves the ground; federal OSHA 6-ft trigger (29 CFR 1926.501), gear homeowner lacks.
- **alternateScenario:** DIY over Professional when task stays at eave height — gutters/downspout/visible crack from a stable ladder, $50–$300 materials, never on the slope.

## njSpecific — What Does NJ Law Require For Roof-Repair Work?
1. HIC registration, no dollar floor, registration-not-a-license (N.J.S.A. 56:8-136).
2. $500 written-contract trigger + required elements (N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2).
3. Homeowner-on-own-home exemption (N.J.S.A. 56:8-140); permits/UCC (N.J.A.C. 5:23) "may still apply."
