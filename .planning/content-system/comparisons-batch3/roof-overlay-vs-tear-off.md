## directAnswer (38 words)
**A tear-off** outlasts **an overlay** — an overlay traps heat that cuts the new shingles' service life ~20–30% (per Angi) — so tear-off wins on lifespan while overlay wins on a ~20–25% / $2,000–$5,000 lower national cost (per HomeGuide).

## verdict
- winner: Tear-off wins on lifespan, deck repair, and warranty; overlay wins on a lower national cost where one sound layer exists.
- reasoning: Comparison-Proposition — **A tear-off** over **an overlay** when the roof stays beyond ~17–20 years (Angi life-haircut math: 25-yr shingle → ~17–20 yr over an overlay).
- alternateScenario: **An overlay** over **a tear-off** when one sound layer exists and budget leads; ~20–25% / $2,000–$5,000 below tear-off (national, HomeGuide/Angi); permitted only per N.J.A.C. 5:23-6.4.
