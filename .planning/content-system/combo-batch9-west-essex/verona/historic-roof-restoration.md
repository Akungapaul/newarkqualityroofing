## Named sources cited (only pack-supported)
- **Secretary of the Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation, Standard 6** — repair-in-kind / match old in design, color, texture, material.
- **NPS Preservation Brief 4** — roof shape/detailing character-defining; document existing roof first.
- **NPS Preservation Brief 19** — red cedar fasteners (zinc-coated/aluminum/stainless, never copper).
- **NPS Preservation Brief 29** — slate: non-ferrous fasteners, ripper + copper strip/slate hook, never coat/seal, durable flashing, **20% repair-vs-replace threshold**.
- **NPS Preservation Brief 30** — clay tile ~100-yr life; failures at fasteners/flashing/sheathing.
- **InterNACHI life-expectancy chart** + **National Slate Association** — slate 60–150 yr / 100-plus premium; copper 70-plus yr.
- **Copper Development Association** — copper service life in excess of 100 years.
- **National Park Service** — National Register listing alone places no restriction on a private owner.
- **HomeGuide** — historic slate restoration $2,500–$10,000+, broken slate $50–$300, clay-tile repair $500–$2,500; NJ ~10–40% above national.
- **N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7** — in-kind reroof = ordinary maintenance path (Afterglow + non-landmark homes).
- **Essex County Parks** — Eagle Rock (First Watchung) + Hilltop (Second Watchung) reservation edges.
