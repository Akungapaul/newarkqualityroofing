## Named sources cited (all in-text)
- **ARMA** — low-pressure 50:50 chlorine-bleach-and-water wash, 15–20-min dwell; pressure-washing causes granule loss + premature failure; moss lifts/curls shingle edges → wind blow-off; maintenance extends shingle life ~25–30%; zinc/copper strips not recommended on an existing roof.
- **ARMA + Atlas Roofing** — Gloeocapsa magma is the most prevalent roof-discoloration algae, feeds on limestone filler.
- **CSSB + NRCA** — north-facing/shaded slopes hold moisture and grow moss faster; NRCA twice-yearly (spring/fall) + post-storm inspection cadence.
- **GAF + InterNACHI** — granule loss >~30% of surface = common rule-of-thumb for beyond repair.
- **InterNACHI life-expectancy chart** — EPDM 15–25 / TPO 7–20 / modified bitumen 20 years (Valley Arts / Main Street low-slope membranes).
- **This Old House** — roof cleaning $300–$1,050 (avg $675 for 1,500 sq ft, $0.20–$0.70/sq ft); moss-prevention treatment +$150–$250 (cost FAQ + pricing.note).
- **N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7 (NJ UCC)** — detached 1–2-family roof-covering cleaning = ordinary maintenance, no permit; enforced by the **City of Orange Township Building & Construction Division**.
- **City of Orange Township Historic Preservation Commission** — COA framing scoped ONLY to the four locally designated districts (Orange Valley, Montrose/Seven Oaks Park, Main Street, St. John's), noting routine cleaning is not a structural alteration; no per-district material rule asserted.
