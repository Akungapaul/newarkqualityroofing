| Claim | Value | Source | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|

> WRITER NOTE: InterNACHI's own inspection guide gives 200-250 (3-tab) vs 250-300 (laminated), with high-end laminated "close to 500." Generic material/cost guides quote architectural ~400-430; that's the heavy/designer end. Safest brand-neutral statement: "architectural shingles weigh more per square than 3-tab (roughly 250-400+ lb vs ~230-250 lb), because they're built from two laminated layers." Frame the extra weight as durability/wind benefit, but note the deck must support it (almost always fine for a like-for-like asphalt re-roof). ARMA does not publish a single canonical per-square weight number — treat exact lb as a range, not a point value.

## 6. NJ CONTEXT — code, wind design, and cost framing

### 6a. Northern-NJ design wind speed (ASCE 7)
| Claim | Value | Source | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northern NJ / Essex County design wind speed (Risk Cat. II) | ~110-115 mph (ASCE 7-16 basic/ultimate wind speed map, Mid-Atlantic) | ASCE 7-16 (referenced by IBC/IRC and NJ UCC) | [PRIMARY-attrib] |
| Implication for shingle choice | a shingle's warranted/classed wind speed should meet or exceed the local design wind speed; architectural (110-130 mph warranty) clears northern-NJ design wind, entry 3-tab (~60 mph) does not | ASCE 7 design principle + manufacturer warranty language | [PRIMARY-attrib] |
> WRITER NOTE: This is the strongest NJ-relevant selling point and it's fully sourced: northern NJ's ~110-115 mph ASCE 7-16 design wind speed lines up almost exactly with the 110-130 mph architectural wind warranty, while a 60 mph 3-tab warranty falls well short. Use ASCE 7-16 by name (current adopted edition; ASCE 7-22 exists but verify NJ UCC adoption before citing — see Gaps).

### 6b. NJ UCC — re-roof is "ordinary maintenance" (no permit)
| Claim | Value | Source | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repair/replacement of existing roof covering on detached 1- & 2-family dwellings = ordinary maintenance | exact regulatory language | N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7 (NJ Uniform Construction Code) | [PRIMARY] |
| No permit, inspection, or notice required for ordinary maintenance | "No permit for, inspections of, or notice to the enforcing agency of ordinary maintenance shall be required." | N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7 | [PRIMARY] |
| Applies REGARDLESS of shingle grade (3-tab or architectural) | ordinary-maintenance status is about the work type, not the product tier | N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7 (interpretation) | [PRIMARY-attrib] |
> WRITER NOTE: Use this verbatim-able fact: in NJ, re-shingling a detached 1-2 family home is ordinary maintenance under N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7 — no permit needed — whether you pick 3-tab or architectural. (Local historic-district/HPC review can still apply in designated districts; that's separate from the UCC permit question.)
