## Direct Answer (hero, 37 words)
**Built-up roofing (BUR)** outlasts **modified bitumen** — BUR's multi-ply gravel-surfaced membrane lasts 30 years versus modified bitumen's 20 (per the InterNACHI chart) — so BUR wins on service life while modified bitumen installs without a hot-asphalt kettle.

## Intro
- Para 1 (R38 entity definitions): BUR = multi-ply "tar and gravel" low-slope membrane (hot-mopped asphalt + reinforcing felts under gravel); modified bitumen = polymer-reinforced asphalt sheet (torch/cold/self-adhered).
- Para 2 (sub-types + failure modes, named-sourced): BUR = 3–5 plies, 30 yr (InterNACHI), surface erosion + ridging. Modified bitumen = SBS/APP grades, 20 yr (InterNACHI), blistering + alligator cracking (UV oxidation) + flashing separation (NRCA).
