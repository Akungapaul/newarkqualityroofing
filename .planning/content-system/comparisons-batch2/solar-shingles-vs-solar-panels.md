# Solar Shingles vs Solar Panels — Answer-First Rewrite (CMP-2)

## Direct answer (hero, 37 words)
**Solar panels** out-produce **solar shingles** per dollar — panels run 20–22% efficient at ~$2.50–$4.00 per watt versus shingles' 14–18% at ~$3.50–$8.00 (per SolarReviews and EnergySage) — so panels win on output and cost, shingles on roof-integrated looks.

## Intro
- BIPV vs BAPV definition (DOE / IEA-PVPS): shingles REPLACE the roof covering; panels are rack-mounted add-ons, power-only.
- Named BIPV products with manufacturer specs: GAF Energy Timberline Solar ES 2 (57 W/shingle, ~16.7 W/sq ft), Tesla Solar Roof (72 W/tile), CertainTeed Solstice (70 W, 19.85% efficiency), SunTegra (105–114 W). Panels = crystalline-silicon ~350–470 W (~400 W common), ~0.5%/yr degradation → ~85–88% at yr 25–30 (NREL).

## Comparison rows (9, all sourced, no `**`)
Efficiency 14–18% vs 20–22% (SolarReviews); cost/W $3.50–$8.00 vs $2.50–$4.00 (EnergySage); cost/sq ft $21–$25 vs $7–$10 (SolarTech Online); roof integration BIPV vs BAPV; penetrations; 6-kW roof area ~360 vs ~250 sq ft (SolarReviews); pairing constraint; power warranty ~25 yr each; NJ SuSI/ADI SREC-II eligibility tie.

## Verdict
Panels win on output-per-dollar and roof area; shingles win on roof-integrated appearance. Comparison-Propositions: panels over shingles when output/$ leads (20–22% @ $2.50–$4.00/W, ~250 sq ft for 6 kW); shingles over panels when a roof needs replacement and integration leads (CertainTeed Solstice cannot go over an existing roof — DOE/CertainTeed).

## Detailed analysis (4 sections)
1. **Which Rooftop Solar Costs Less Per Watt?** — panels $2.50–$4.00/W vs shingles $3.50–$8.00/W (~1.5–2×), EnergySage/SolarReviews/SolarTech Online; explicit note that no primary cost authority sets a fixed NJ price.
2. **Which Rooftop Solar Produces More From Limited Roof Area?** — 20–22% vs 14–18%, ~250 vs ~360 sq ft for 6 kW (SolarReviews); flush-mount thermal penalty ~0.3–0.5%/°C above 25°C (WattBuild/NREL).
3. **Which Rooftop Solar Integrates Into the Roof?** — BIPV nails in as roof covering; BAPV on flashed lag-bolt rails; panels SHADE the covering, do NOT extend roof life (§12, NRCA/DOE).
4. **Which Rooftop Solar Holds NJ Weather Ratings?** — UL 7103 / UL 790 Class A / UL 2218 Class 4 / ASTM D3161 Class F; NEC 690.12 rapid shutdown (≤30 V outside / ≤80 V inside, 30 s); ratings attributed to manufacturers, not NQR-verified.

## NJ-specific
SuSI/ADI SREC-II (15-yr term, NJBPU, qualitative value → refer to NJ Clean Energy Program current rate); net metering N.J.S.A. 48:3-87 (1:1 up to annual usage, surplus at wholesale); federal §25D credit was 30% through 2025, repealed under P.L. 119-21 (IRS) — refer to a tax professional.

## Residential / Commercial
Residential: shingles suit integration during a reroof; panels suit output on a sound roof; re-roof-first rule of thumb (NREL). Commercial: panels fit (ballasted/mechanically-attached racking per ASCE 7, NRCA/SPRI); BIPV rarely fits flat commercial roofs.

## FAQs (5)
SREC-II eligibility; existing-roof constraint; output per sq ft; the repealed federal credit (currency fix); panels-don't-extend-roof-life (§12).
