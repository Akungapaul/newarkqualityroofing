## Hero answer (directAnswer, 39w counting em-dash tokens)
**Spray polyurethane foam (SPF)** leads NJ total energy performance — its aged R-6.0 to R-6.5 per inch (ICC-ES/ASTM C1289 LTTR, SPFA) adds insulation, while **white TPO/PVC membranes** lead reflectance at ~0.70–0.85 solar reflectance, per the CRRC.

## introHeading (question, no bold)
What Roofing Materials Are Most Energy-Efficient for New Jersey?

## The two leading energy levers, by function (R38)
- **SPF** = the conductive-insulation lever (only covering here that adds R-value).
- **White single-ply membrane (TPO/PVC)** = the reflectance lever (lowers surface temp).
- Contenders defined at first mention: **reflective metal roofing**, **cool-roof asphalt shingles** (reflective-granule lines), **green/vegetated roofs** (rated only for 5–40-yr life, InterNACHI), **natural slate** (no sourced reflectance — not a reflectance lever).

## Verdict (ranking statement)
- **winner (25w):** SPF leads total NJ energy performance via added R-value; white TPO/PVC lead low-slope reflectance (SPFA, CRRC).
- **reasoning:** SPF is the only added-R-value covering — aged R-6.0–6.5/in — addressing Newark's heating-dominated Zone 4A–5 where R-value governs winter heat loss (DOE).
- **alternateScenario:** White single-ply leads low-slope cooling — ~0.70–0.85 SR cuts peak cooling demand 11–27% in AC buildings (EPA); cool-roof asphalt is the lower-cost steep-slope path.

## Key sourced figures used
- SPF aged **R-6.0 to R-6.5 per inch** — ICC-ES/ASTM C1289 LTTR; SPFA (facts-materials-economics §6).
- SPF foam layer **30+ years** when coated — SPFA.
- White PVC/TPO **~0.70–0.85 solar reflectance, ~0.80–0.90 thermal emittance**, ASTM C1549, CRRC (facts-materials-economics §6).
- **EPA: reduce PEAK cooling demand 11–27%** in air-conditioned residential buildings.
- **DOE: reflective roof stays >50°F cooler** than conventional; conventional roof framing per DOE.
- **LBNL: clean white roof (80% reflective) ~55°F/31°C cooler** than gray (20%).
- Cool roofs defined by **reflectance + emittance per CRRC-1, NOT R-value**; coatings add no R-value (DOE/CRRC/RCMA).
- **ENERGY STAR roof program ended June 1, 2022; CRRC-1 successor** (EPA/CRRC/SPRI).
- **2021 IECC Table R402.1.3 ceiling R-60 (Zones 4 & 5), R-49 raised-heel exception**; NJ adopted 2021 IECC April 2023 (ICC/NJ DCA).
- NJ heating-climate caveat (winter heating penalty; net benefit climate+insulation dependent) — DOE/EPA.
- **§25D solar + §25C insulation repealed after 2025-12-31** (P.L. 119-21/OBBB); §25D was 30% through 2025 historically; **§179D commercial deduction** (not a roof credit), repealed for construction after 2026-06-30 — IRS.
- **SuSI/SREC-II via NJBPU, 15-yr term** (qualitative, no $/MWh).
- NJ solar **sales-tax exemption N.J.S.A. 54:32B-8.33; property-tax exemption N.J.S.A. 54:4-3.113a/b** (NJ Division of Taxation).
- **IRC R806.2 attic vent 1/150** (1/300 cold-zone leg N/A Newark Zone 4–5); ~50/50 intake/exhaust (IRC/ARMA/Air Vent Inc.).
- **Green/vegetated roof 5–40-yr life** — InterNACHI chart (lifespan only).
