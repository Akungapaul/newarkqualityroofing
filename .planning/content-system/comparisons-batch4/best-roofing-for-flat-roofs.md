## detailedAnalysis (4 facets, all question headings, R3 strict body↔lead bold)
1. **Which Flat Roof Membrane Handles Ponding Water Best?** — TPO/PVC thermoplastic resist standing water; EPDM next; SPF erodes. NRCA ¼ in/ft (~2%) positive drainage; ponding accelerates deterioration (NRCA/ARMA/SPFA).
2. **Which Flat Roof Seam Is the Most Reliable?** — heat-welded TPO/PVC strongest (NRCA); EPDM adhesive/tape weakest (seam separation = dominant EPDM failure mode); modified bitumen between.
3. **Which Flat Roof Membrane Stays Coolest in Summer?** — TPO/PVC white 0.70–0.85 SR / 0.80–0.90 TE (CRRC/ASTM C1549); EPA 11–27% peak cooling; DOE >50°F cooler; Zone 4A–5 winter offset. EPDM black absorbs; SPF reflectance only via coating.
4. **Which Flat Roof Lasts Longest in NJ?** — BUR 30 yr, PVC 20–30, EPDM 15–25, modified bitumen 20, TPO 7–20 (InterNACHI/SPRI); SPF 30+ coated, recoat 10–20 yr.

## njSpecific — What Does NJ Code Require on a Flat Roof?
Positive drainage (NRCA ¼ in/ft ~2%) via tapered insulation/structural slope; 2021 IRC adopted via N.J.A.C. 5:23 bars recover over unsound deck (IRC R908 / N.J.A.C. 5:23-6.4); Zone 4A–5, 31.5" snow (NOAA), ~35–45 freeze-thaw; no NJ cool-roof prescriptive mandate for low-slope residential.

## residentialSection — Which Flat Roof Suits an Essex County House?
EPDM ($7.00–$10.00/sq ft, freeze-thaw flexible) + TPO ($8.00–$12.00/sq ft, reflective white) for porches/additions/garages; membrane with correct slope/flashing/drainage ends asphalt-on-low-slope leaks.

## commercialSection — Which Flat Roof Fits a Commercial Building?
TPO reflectance → EPA 11–27% peak cooling on A/C buildings; PVC grease/chemical resistance for restaurants (20–30 yr, SPRI); modified bitumen multi-ply for foot traffic (20 yr).
