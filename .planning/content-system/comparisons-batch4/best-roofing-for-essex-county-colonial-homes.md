## directAnswer (38 w)
**Architectural asphalt shingles** rank first for most Essex County Colonials at $6.50–$11.00 per NJ square foot (Josten Roofing); **natural slate** ranks first for a historic Colonial as the in-kind material under the Secretary of the Interior's Standard 6.

## Key sourced figures used
- Architectural asphalt: **30-year** life (InterNACHI); **$6.50–$11.00/sq ft NJ** (Josten Roofing); within the **$10,000–$25,000** NJ full-replacement benchmark (HomeAdvisor/Modernize NJ).
- Natural slate: **60–150 years**, premium **100+** (InterNACHI / National Slate Association); **$10–$30/sq ft NJ** (NJ roofing guides §7); repair-vs-replace at **20%** broken/missing slates (Brief 29); non-ferrous (copper/stainless) fasteners only (Brief 29); not coated/walked-on; salvageable slates reused (Standard 6 / Brief 29).
- Standing seam metal: **40–80 years** (InterNACHI); **$9.00–$16.00/sq ft NJ** (Josten Roofing); sheds snow; standing/batten/flat seam systems (CDA); Brief 4 names tin/terne/copper/zinc as traditional Colonial metals.
- Cedar shingle: shingle **30–50 years** (Cedar Shake & Shingle Bureau); red cedar requires hot-dipped zinc/aluminum/stainless nails — **not copper** (Brief 19, Sharon C. Park, AIA).
- Synthetic (simulated) slate: **10–35 years**, composite lines designed **40–50** (InterNACHI / CertainTeed).
- Copper: **70+** (InterNACHI); service life **in excess of 100 years** properly installed (Copper Development Association); flashing failure a major cause of roof deterioration (Brief 4).
- Climate: Newark **31.5 in** average annual snowfall (NOAA 1991–2020); **~35–45** freeze-thaw cycles (regional estimate); **110–115 mph** design wind speed, ASCE 7-16.
- Code: ordinary-maintenance reroof exemption for detached 1–2-family (N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7); 25%/12-month threshold on other structures (5:23-2.7(c)); Rehabilitation Subcode complete-removal rule for wood-shake/slate/clay-tile + no third layer (5:23-6.4); local-district COA gate (N.J.S.A. 40:55D-107); Register listing places no private-owner restriction (National Park Service).
- Substyle period materials (qualitative architectural facts, kept): Georgian (1700–1780) wood shingle/slate; Federal (1780–1820) slate/metal; Colonial Revival (1880–1955) → architectural asphalt. Per NPS Preservation Brief 4.
