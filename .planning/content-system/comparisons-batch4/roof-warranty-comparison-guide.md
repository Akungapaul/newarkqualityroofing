## Ranked options (warranty STRUCTURE — no brand is ranked)
1. **Manufacturer system warranty** — material defect + certified workmanship; non-prorated window. GAF Golden Pledge used as a NEUTRAL manufacturer-attributed example only.
2. **Manufacturer material-only warranty** — defective materials only, prorated after a 10–15-yr non-prorated window; no labor/workmanship.
3. **Contractor workmanship warranty** — install defects only; commonly 1–10 yrs, no industry-mandated minimum; ends if the contractor closes.
4. **Commercial No-Dollar-Limit (NDL) guarantee** — whole system edge-to-edge, material + labor, no dollar cap; 5–30 yrs.
5. **Commercial system warranty (non-NDL)** — 100% material + labor + workmanship but capped at original installed cost (OIC).
6. **Transferable warranty** — transfers once to first buyer within a manufacturer window (CertainTeed: within 15 yrs).

## Direct answer (hero, 36 words)
A non-prorated **manufacturer system warranty** ranks highest for homes and a commercial **No-Dollar-Limit (NDL) guarantee** ranks highest for low-slope buildings — both cover material and workmanship, unlike a **contractor workmanship warranty** alone, per NRCA and GAF.

## Intro heading (question)
Which Roofing Warranty Gives an Essex County Owner the Strongest Protection?

## Verdict
- **winner (ranking statement, structural — no brand):** A non-prorated manufacturer system warranty installed by a credentialed contractor gives the strongest residential protection; a commercial NDL guarantee leads for low-slope buildings.
- **reasoning:** manufacturer-set/registered → material coverage survives the installer closing; GAF Golden Pledge example = 50-yr material / 25-yr workmanship, 10-yr non-prorated (NRCA, GAF).
- **alternateScenario:** commercial NDL = whole system edge-to-edge, no dollar cap vs base material-only that prorates + caps at OIC (GAF, Johns Manville).

## Detailed analysis (4 facets, each lead ≤40w, strict body↔lead bold)
1. **How Does Pro-Rated Coverage Differ From Non-Prorated Coverage?** — non-prorated window (10–15 yrs limited-lifetime asphalt, NRCA); system = full-replacement value in window (GAF Golden Pledge 50-yr non-prorated material + 25-yr workmanship + tear-off/disposal); material-only prorates by age, excludes labor (NRCA/Cobex/Indy Roof).
2. **Why Does a Manufacturer Warranty Outlast a Contractor Workmanship Warranty?** — manufacturer-set survives contractor closure (NRCA, NRCA Roofing Manual, InterNACHI/IIBEC); workmanship = install defects (welded seams, flashing, fasteners) 1–10 yrs no mandated minimum (NRCA, Owens Corning).
3. **What Voids a Roofing Warranty?** — attic ventilation = most-cited void cause (GAF); IRC R806.2 net free area 1/150 in Newark (1/300 cold-zone leg N/A), InterNACHI; alterations + deferred maintenance void under owner-responsibility/Scheduled Maintenance Checklist terms; improper solar/satellite flashing voids that damage (GAF).
4. **How Does a Commercial No-Dollar-Limit Warranty Work?** — NDL = labor + material, no cap vs base material-only prorated + capped at OIC (GAF, JM); edge-to-edge (membrane, base flashing, insulation, expansion-joint covers, metal flashings), 5–30 yrs; manufacturer-issued after authorized install + inspection, ≥14-day advance notice, valid via inspections/maintenance/records + 30-day written leak notice (JM, GAF).

## NJ-specific (the local hook)
**What Does NJ Law Require a Contractor to Disclose About a Warranty?**
- Warranty terms in signed written contract for jobs over $500 — N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)12, "any guarantee or warranty" is an enumerated element (NJ Consumer Fraud Act).
- Annual HIC registration, NJ Division of Consumer Affairs, Contractors' Registration Act N.J.S.A. 56:8-136, NO dollar threshold — a registration, NOT a license; disputes route through Office of Consumer Protection.
- "Limited" warranty = Magnuson-Moss labeling term-of-art (conditioned by prorating, owner-maintenance, exclusions), 15 U.S.C. §2301.

## Residential
**Which Warranty Suits an Essex County House?** — manufacturer system for long-hold (GAF Golden Pledge example 50/25); transfers once within manufacturer window (CertainTeed SureStart PLUS fully transferable if sold within 15 yrs, NRCIA); contractor workmanship as a layer only (1–10 yrs, ends at closure).

## Commercial
**Which Warranty Fits a Commercial Building?** — NDL fits low-slope, edge-to-edge no cap vs non-NDL system capped at OIC (GAF, JM); single-ply TPO/EPDM/PVC + bituminous 5–30 yrs through manufacturer-designated contractor + final inspection (JM, GAF); valid only via documented annual inspections + scheduled maintenance records (GAF Diamond Pledge owner-responsibility).

## FAQs (5)
1. What does a "lifetime" roofing warranty actually mean? → describes manufacturer's repair/replace obligation, NOT a roof-life promise; non-prorated only for a window (10–15 yrs asphalt limited-lifetime), then prorates (NRCA).
2. Is a roofing warranty transferable if I sell my NJ home? → transfers once within manufacturer window; CertainTeed SureStart PLUS within 15 yrs; standard terms reduce/limit later-owner coverage (SureStart PLUS brochure, NRCIA).
3. Does a manufacturer warranty cover storm damage? → covers material/workmanship defects, not storm (homeowner insurance); "limited" = conditioned per Magnuson-Moss (15 U.S.C. §2301).
4. What is a No-Dollar-Limit warranty on a commercial roof? → labor + material, no monetary cap; non-NDL caps at OIC; NDL removes cap edge-to-edge (GAF, JM).
5. Does NJ require a contractor to put the warranty in writing? → yes, for jobs over $500; N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)12 lists "any guarantee or warranty" as a required element (NJ Consumer Fraud Act).
