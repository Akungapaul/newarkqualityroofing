## directAnswer (35 words)
**TPO** and **EPDM** lead NJ commercial low-slope roofing — white **TPO** heat-welds its seams and reflects sun at ~0.70–0.85 solar reflectance, while **EPDM** runs the lowest single-ply install cost, per the CRRC and Josten Roofing.

## verdict
- **winner:** TPO + EPDM lead NJ single-ply (TPO = welded-seam strength + reflectance; EPDM = lowest cost); PVC/metal/SPF lead narrower scenarios.
- **reasoning:** TPO leads air-conditioned buildings — welded seams + ~0.70–0.85 reflectance cut peak cooling demand 11–27% (CRRC, EPA) at $8–$12/sq ft NJ (Josten Roofing).
- **alternateScenario:** EPDM leads budget warehouses ($7–$10/sq ft); PVC leads grease-exposed restaurant roofs (20–30 yr); standing seam metal leads long-hold sloped properties (40–80 yr).

## detailedAnalysis (4 facets — cost / lifespan / ponding+failure-mode / cooling)
1. **Which Commercial Membrane Costs Less to Install in NJ?** — EPDM $7–$10 lowest, TPO $8–$12, PVC $6–$12 (clusters $8–$12), SPF $4–$8. Sources: Josten Roofing (NJ TPO/EPDM); M&M Roofing/WeatherStar (PVC, SPF); ICC-ES/SPFA (SPF R-6.0–6.5/in).
2. **Which Commercial Roof Lasts Longest in NJ?** — metal 40–80 (copper 70+), PVC 20–30, BUR 30, modified bitumen 20, EPDM 15–25. Sources: InterNACHI chart; Single Ply Roofing Industry; GAF EverGuard. EPDM failure = seam separation (NRCA-attrib).
3. **Which Commercial Roof Handles Ponding and Failure Modes Best?** — TPO/PVC heat-welded seams resist ponding; EPDM adhesive-seam separation; modified bitumen blistering/alligator cracking; NRCA ¼-in/ft min slope. Sources: NRCA, ARMA, InterNACHI.
4. **Which Commercial Roof Cuts Summer Cooling Demand Most?** — white TPO/PVC ~0.70–0.85 SR / ~0.80–0.90 TE (ASTM C1549, CRRC); EPA 11–27% PEAK cooling demand; DOE >50°F cooler; Zone 4A–5 winter-heating offset (DOE); no R-value from reflectance (CRRC).

## residentialSection — Which Commercial Membrane Suits a Mixed-Use Building?
